Focused support for physical products.

I support mechanical engineering projects from design and structural analysis to integration and testing. Engagements can cover a specific technical question or a defined part of the hardware-development process.

My strongest experience is with physical products, composite structures, mechanical systems and hardware that must move from CAD into manufacturing and assembly.

Mechanical Design

Design mechanical parts, assemblies and interfaces from a defined requirement through manufacturing-ready documentation.

Typical work

  • CAD parts and assemblies
  • Mounting systems and interfaces
  • Packaging and access studies
  • Design updates from manufacturing feedback

Typical deliverables

  • CAD models
  • Assembly layouts
  • Interface definitions
  • Technical drawings
  • Design recommendations

Typical work

Typical deliverables

  • Integration layouts

  • Integration recommendations

Relevant work

Structural Analysis

Assess stiffness, deformation and structural response under defined loads, including composite structures.

Typical work

  • Linear and nonlinear FEA
  • Composite and laminate assessment
  • Stiffness, load paths and critical regions
  • Design iteration from analysis results

Typical deliverables

  • Analysis assumptions
  • Structural models and results
  • Critical-region assessment
  • Design recommendations

Relevant work

  • Full-Vessel Composite Structural Analysis — EPFL Solar Boat
  • Hydrofoil Structural Analysis — Navier

Test & Simulation Correlation

Use physical measurements to check model behaviour, investigate discrepancies and identify the data needed for reliable engineering decisions.

Typical work

  • Measurement and test definition
  • Instrumentation and test setups
  • Predicted versus measured behaviour
  • Discrepancy and uncertainty analysis

Typical deliverables

  • Test plan
  • Instrumentation definition
  • Model / measurement comparison
  • Uncertainty assessment
  • Recommended next measurements or model updates

Relevant work

  • Simulation / Test Correlation — Navier
